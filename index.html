ABOUT US

THE PASSION, STRENGTH AND SOUL OF ARGENTINA, ON STAGE FOR THE WORLD.

A POWERFUL SHOW. A UNIQUE CULTURAL EXPERIENCE.

MALAMBO KING is an Argentine dance and entertainment company founded in 2017 in Tucumán, Argentina, and directed by renowned dancers and choreographers Romina García Fleury and Nicolás Correa.

With a professional cast of 20 talented artists, we create spectacular productions that bring the passion, strength and soul of Argentina to international stages.

Our performances combine the explosive energy of Malambo, the elegance and sensuality of Tango, the powerful rhythms of traditional Bombo drums, the spectacular skills of Boleadoras and the richness of Argentine folklore dance.

Every performance is designed to captivate audiences through exceptional technique, dynamic choreography, striking costumes, powerful rhythms and an unforgettable stage presence.

AN INTERNATIONAL SHOW FOR EVERY VENUE

MALAMBO KING has represented Argentine culture across Europe, Asia, Africa, the Middle East and the Americas, performing at international festivals, entertainment productions, artistic seasons and prestigious events.

We offer versatile productions adapted to different formats and audiences, including:

Theatre and live entertainment

Special guest performances

Whether you are looking for a spectacular full-stage production or an exclusive performance by a professional duo, MALAMBO KING delivers a distinctive artistic experience tailored to your production.

ARTISTIC PROFILES

Meet the artistic directors and principal dancers behind MALAMBO KING.

ROMINA GARCÍA FLEURY

ARTISTIC DIRECTOR · CHOREOGRAPHER · PRINCIPAL DANCER

A dancer and choreographer with a solid academic background and extensive international experience, Romina García Fleury began her dance training at the age of seven.

She graduated as a Classical Dance Teacher from the Higher School of Artistic Education of Tucumán. In 2000, she was selected through an audition to receive a scholarship from the prestigious Julio Bocca Foundation , one of the leading institutions in Argentine dance.

She continued her professional development under distinguished artists associated with the Teatro Colón in Buenos Aires , subsequently broadening her training in tango, Argentine folklore dance, flamenco, Spanish and Arabic dances, and Latin American rhythms.

She also holds a qualification as an Argentine Folklore Dance Teacher from the Eduardo Albistur Conservatory in Buenos Aires.

Her career includes performances, tours and educational activities throughout Europe, including a tour of Italy and France in 2017, during which she taught classes and performed in tango and folklore dance productions.

Her classical training, combined with an in-depth knowledge of Argentina's popular dance traditions, gives MALAMBO KING a distinctive choreographic identity defined by elegance, technical precision and expressive power.

NICOLÁS CORREA

ARTISTIC DIRECTOR · CHOREOGRAPHER · PRINCIPAL DANCER

A leading exponent of Argentine malambo, Nicolás Correa began his artistic training at the age of three and has built an outstanding career, earning major awards at some of Argentina's most important folklore festivals.

A qualified Argentine Folklore Dance Teacher and graduate of the Eduardo Albistur Conservatory in Buenos Aires, he has received, among other distinctions, the following titles:

  • 1997 — Argentine Children's Malambo Champion, Laborde, Córdoba.
  • 2006 — Argentine Northern-Style Malambo Champion, Baradero, Buenos Aires.
  • 2014 — Individual Malambo Champion, Cosquín National Folklore Festival.
  • 2015 — Argentine Southern-Style Malambo Champion, Baradero.
  • 2015 — Cosquín Group Malambo Champion.
  • 2015 — Outstanding New Talent of the Cosquín National Folklore Festival.

His talent gained widespread television exposure when he won ShowMatch , the renowned television programme hosted by Marcelo Tinelli, with a spectacular production featuring bombo drums and malambo. He also reached the final of Talento Argentino , presenting a large-scale choreographic production featuring twenty performers on stage.

Since 2006, he has pursued an intensive international career, touring Italy, France, the United States of America and Canada. Among his notable engagements was a two-month season in France in 2012, during which he gave more than thirty theatrical performances.

2024, he won the competition to become Director of the Folklore Dance Ballet of the Province of Tucumán , one of the leading ensembles representing the province's folk traditions.

His extraordinary command of malambo, his experience as a choreographer and his ability to conceive large-scale stage productions are central elements of MALAMBO KING's artistic identity.

International Experience

Our Journey Around The World

MALAMBO KING has established an international track record through performances at major festivals, entertainment productions and artistic seasons across different continents. Representing Argentine culture on international stages, our journey reflects artistic excellence, dedication and passion.

International Destinations

The Middle East

Saudi Arabia — Riyadh Season

Participation in Riyadh Season, including Boulevard World in 2023 and Eid al-Fitr celebrations.

Saudi Arabia — NAWAL NOW

In 2022, the company opened the international show NAWAL NOW, starring Kuwaiti singer Nawal El Kuwaiti and produced by Rotana.

United Arab Emirates — Sheikh Zayed Festival

Performances at the Sheikh Zayed Festival in Abu Dhabi in 2019 and 2021.

Qatar — Sealine Festival

Performances at the Sealine Festival in Doha, January 2025.

Lebanon

Artistic seasons and performances at private events, celebrations and hotel venues during 2023 and 2024.

Asia

China — Sanya and Maoming

Extended artistic seasons in China, performing at international hotel establishments.

In 2024, a season in Sanya ran from February to October. In 2025, the company returned to China for a new season in Maoming, Guangdong Province, with performances at hotel chains including Sheraton and Wyndham.

Africa

Egypt — Sharm el-Sheikh and Marsa Alam

Artistic seasons at five-star hotels in Sharm el-Sheikh and Marsa Alam in 2019, 2020, 2022 and 2024.

In 2019, the company was invited to stage the opening performance of the 48th edition of MISS INTERCONTINENTAL, held in Sharm el-Sheikh.

Europe

France and Italy

Nicolás Correa performed in Italy from 2006 onwards and undertook a notable tour of France in 2012, giving more than thirty performances over two months.

Romina García Fleury also carried out artistic and educational activities in both countries in 2017.

Türkiye — Antalya

In 2021, MALAMBO KING undertook an extensive artistic season from May to October, with performances at more than fifty hotel establishments in Antalya.

The Americas

United States of America and Canada

The careers of the company's directors include artistic tours in the United States and Canada.

Argentina

Performances at festivals and celebrations across several provinces, including Córdoba and Neuquén.

Bolivia and Chile

Participation in cultural festivals and events, including the Tarija Chacarera Festival, the Bermejo International Lapacho Festival and the Latin American Cultural Integration Gathering in Caldera.

Ukraine — A Particularly Significant Artistic Presence

MALAMBO KING's international career includes a distinguished presence in Ukraine, where its artists have taken part in cultural activities since 2020.

Following its initial virtual presentations and the first live performances in 2021, the company became the centrepiece of a series of events dedicated to Argentine culture in Kyiv.

2024 — First Edition

Presentation of "An Evening of Argentine Tango and Malambo", organised by the Embassy of Argentina at the National Operetta Theatre of Ukraine in Kyiv.

The visit also included public performances in Irpin and at the Ukrainian Chamber of Commerce and Industry.

2025 — Second Edition

The company returned to Kyiv for a second edition of the show, further strengthening its recognition among Ukrainian audiences.

2026 — Third Edition

On 12 November 2026, Romina García Fleury and Nicolás Correa returned to the National Operetta Theatre of Ukraine in Kyiv to headline the third edition of this production, bringing together outstanding Argentine and Ukrainian performers in a celebration of music, tango and the artistic traditions of both countries.

The third edition further strengthened the cultural connection between Argentina and Ukraine, reaffirming the power of art to build bridges between nations and bring audiences of different nationalities closer together.

Awards and Recognition

2018

The MALAMBO KING show was declared of cultural and legislative interest by the Honourable Legislature of the Province of Tucumán, in recognition of its contribution to promoting and preserving Argentine traditions.

2024

The company's growth enabled it to operate three artistic ensembles simultaneously at different international destinations, demonstrating its production capacity, organisational strength and commercial expansion.
